# Research Experiment

Own the requested experiment or its operational support. The project-local
`research-task` skill owns rctl lifecycle and machine acceptance; runner records
own execution evidence. Choose methods and work order within the agreement.
Identify the requested operation first. For a rapid test, training setup/diagnosis
or reusable runner guidance, use the matching reference below and its completion
contract. The scientific agreement below applies to claim-carrying execution.

## Before claim-carrying execution

Reuse the supplied protocol, project question and related evidence. Fix the
question, comparison purpose, intervention, named control versions, inputs/data
and split, information/feedback boundaries, primary endpoint/evaluator,
aggregation/selection rule, budget, stopping conditions and expected evidence.
State what favorable, unfavorable and inconclusive evidence changes.

Give complete-method comparisons comparable starting inputs, tools and total
effort; keep generated information in its producing arm. Shared-information
ablations and enhanced controls retain separate identities and interpretations.
Keep development feedback distinct from confirmation and use applicable project
comparison guidance.

Justify scale for the intended decision: independent cases/clusters, repeats,
meaningful effect/precision or deterministic coverage, assumptions and executable
budget. Statistical superiority, noninferiority or equivalence needs appropriate
uncertainty/planning; a supplied reproduction retains its protocol and scope.
If the allowance cannot support the intended claim, narrow it before execution.

For rctl-managed work, read the project-local research-task skill and use its
native contract. The [contract example](assets/experiment-contract.md) supplies
optional domain detail; preserve generated frontmatter, goal contribution and
designated review when declared. Retain the agreement with `begin` before
claim-carrying runs and amend material changes before dependent work. Outside a
managed project, retain the requested protocol or bounded note without installing
rctl merely for a pilot. A different scientific question needs its own agreement.

## Before verification and closure

Inspect actual runner evidence: commands/configs/code/environment, inputs, outputs,
health, failed/null runs, achieved coverage, aggregation and governing revisions.
Link runner-owned facts rather than duplicate a run diary. A launch or completed
process does not establish scientific validity.

Deliver the requested result or operational handoff. An experimental result states
the primary finding, comparability, uncertainty, deviations, claims supported/not
made and next decision; managed work uses the native result. No significant gain
does not establish equivalence; a budget stop can leave the evidence inconclusive.
Keep scientific assessment separate from criterion verdict and investment choice.

For managed work, use research-task to supply required reviews (including goal
impact when declared), verify accepted criteria and close only with a current pass.
Generate analysis artifacts before verification; checks read prepared evidence.
Promote established findings with scope/source links after closure. Other work
delivers its requested result without claiming an rctl lifecycle transition.

For problem validation, a null result bounds the tested mechanism and construction
budget. Preserve the authorized object and contribution. Legacy task summaries
remain historical evidence; research-task's migration reference governs requested
live migration. Advice and bounded mechanical edits may remain inline.
