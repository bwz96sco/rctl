# Rapid tests

Obtain evidence that changes a research decision. Choose the smallest useful
investigation, its methods and iteration order within the agreed scope and budget.

## Before execution

Use the supplied question/idea and relevant prior evidence. Record a compact bet
in the existing task or trial note: what uncertainty matters, the observation that
distinguishes the possibilities, comparison/measurement conditions, decision rule,
evidence locations and time/call allowance. A smaller test preserves the scientific
distinction and explains what it decides about the parent claim.

Fix the relevant control/version, information and feedback boundaries, primary
endpoint, independent units/repeats and interpretation of positive, negative and
inconclusive observations. Size the test for that decision and its allowance;
mechanism demonstrations and population claims need different evidence. Use
applicable project comparison guidance. A failure-selected control draw is
admission evidence; disclose selection and use fresh comparable draws when
estimating intervention benefit.

Use user/project limits. For an otherwise unbounded first observation or candidate
screen, propose a small allowance; the earlier 20-minute preparation, two-hour
first observation and four-hour total can be starting estimates, not universal
limits. A method-stage pilot uses its established evaluator, controls and stage
budget. Retry/stop rules follow the bet's uncertainty, evidence and remaining allowance.
Carry consumed time/calls across resumptions; scope changes do not renew them.

In an rctl-managed project, use research-task's `references/rapid-trials.md` for
the native contract before launch unless the user chooses note-only tracking.
Reuse the bet rather than create a second plan. Outside a managed project, a trial
note suffices. Discussion and read-only inspection need no new managed task.

## Before reporting completion

Inspect the actual input, operation/contrast, observation and inference. Retain
all tried outcomes, assistance, failures and actual spending. Report the primary
endpoint, relevant regressions/costs and what comparison was actually performed.
Keep generated method information inside its producing arm for complete-method
comparisons; shared information estimates a conditional ablation. Label enhanced
controls separately and retain earlier outcomes.

Distinguish evidence from the investment decision: no observed opportunity,
insufficient precision and infrastructure failure have different meanings. State
whether to continue, revise or stop, with the evidence and remaining uncertainty.
A revision needs an observed failure, a specific expected delta and remaining
allowance. A stop in spending does not by itself refute the scientific premise.

Deliver the compact bet, result, costs and decision in the existing note, such as
`rapid-test.md`, linked to raw evidence. For a managed trial, write its native
result and verify/close through research-task; negative or bounded inconclusive
findings can complete the task. Further investment follows existing authorization.

## References when needed

For a requested formal comparison, use the experiment contract details. Read the shared
[computation checks](computation-checks.md) for an
unresolved numerical-validity question. Add a check only for a named failure that
would change the result or next action. Keep scoped findings in existing project
guidance with their evidence; live progress remains in the task and runner.
