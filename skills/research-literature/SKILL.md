# Research Literature

Read the papers that matter to a target question and record what each paper establishes and where its evidence fails. Paper-pool construction belongs to `$paper-discovery`; cross-paper argument belongs to `$research-synthesis`.

## Workspace

Read the project-relative `vault` binding in `.rctl/project.json`; when unbound, retain the project's existing note convention. Reuse an existing topic directory; for a new one use `<vault>/literature/<topic-slug>/`, or `artifacts/research-literature/<topic-slug>/` without a note root. Read the repository-level `zotero-collection.md` when present. Keep `register.md` and `notes/<paper-id>.md` in the topic directory. PDFs stay in an ignored local archive.

## Workflow

1. **Lock the question and corpus.** Put the user's target question verbatim at the top of `register.md`, followed by supplied scope constraints. Use the existing register and project collection or the exact paper or fixed corpus supplied by the user. Route requests for new candidates, citation snowballing, or collection updates to `$paper-discovery`, then resume from its register.
2. **Select decisive papers.** Select papers capable of changing the answer, closest-prior comparison, or evaluation boundary. For a named paper, stay with that paper. For an existing corpus, prioritize retained `core` and `closest_candidate` rows; do not expand the corpus inside the reading pass.
3. **Establish access.** Resolve each selected row to its Zotero item, supplied file, or stable source, and identify the exact version being read. Record access as `full_pdf`, `partial_text`, `abstract_only`, or `unavailable`; preserve the row's `metadata_status` as `verified`, `conflicting`, or `unverified` rather than re-running discovery. Inherit its publication, affiliation, and collection-membership provenance with source dates. Where provenance is missing for a directly supplied paper or legacy row, record metadata supported by the supplied source and mark external verification or membership `unchecked`; create a minimal register row when necessary. Send requests to save or refresh collection records to `$paper-discovery`.
4. **Reuse before rereading.** Reuse an existing full-paper note when its version, question coverage, and evidence anchors are adequate. For an isolated missing or disputed claim, check the relevant source sections together with needed definitions and evaluation context, then amend that claim and record the checked scope. For full-paper assignments, read any paper lacking an adequate full-paper note. Otherwise reread only when a material version or question change exceeds a local check, contradictions cannot be resolved locally, or the user requests a fresh independent reading. Metadata or template changes alone do not justify rereading.
5. **Read papers independently when needed.** Use one clean subagent per paper. Default full-paper readers to `gpt-5.6-luna` with reasoning effort `max`, honoring an explicit user override. Where the host supports these controls, set them explicitly and start without conversation history (`fork_turns: "none"`); report an unavailable requested configuration rather than silently substituting it. Give the reader only the available paper text, target question, complete register row and corresponding provenance, and [note-template.md](note-template.md). Reuse a page-preserving text extraction, consult PDF pages for unclear equations or figures, and keep the note concise without repeating the paper section by section. Save the returned note as `notes/<paper-id>.md`. Partial or abstract-only reading remains `skimmed`, with deeper claims marked not assessable. If a concrete proof or experimental interpretation remains unresolved after source checks, escalate that bounded issue with its source context to a more capable model; this is not an automatic second full reading.
6. **Close the reading state.** Update access, status, decision, and relevance without changing Zotero identity or search-coverage history. Reconcile each note's bibliographic and membership fields against the supplied provenance; flag newly found discrepancies for `$paper-discovery` instead of silently replacing verified records with PDF claims. `read` means the full paper was read, including a documented earlier reading; a targeted check alone never promotes `skimmed` to `read`. Otherwise use `skimmed` or `blocked`. Every material claim or defect in a completed note needs an evidence basis, confidence or caveat, and section/page/figure/table anchor. Include the template's concise evidence-quality assessment for the target question.
7. **Route the result.** Send newly exposed missing papers to `$paper-discovery`. When the user asks to compare papers, resolve contradictions, map the field, identify closest prior work, or answer across the corpus, invoke `$research-synthesis` after the decisive notes are ready.

For a full-paper assignment, complete when every requested or decisive `core` or `closest_candidate` paper is full-text read or explicitly blocked; adequate existing full-paper notes count. For a targeted claim check, complete when the requested claim is supported, corrected, or explicitly unresolved with the checked scope recorded. Every read paper has a skeleton note tied to the version read and provenance, and every material claim or defect has an evidence basis, confidence or caveat, and section/page/figure/table anchor. The note must state what the evidence supports for this question and its main limitation; unresolved publication or group information alone does not prevent reading. Cross-paper requests complete through `$research-synthesis`.

## Evidence boundary

- Keep author-stated limitations, observed failures, and analyst-inferred defects separate.
- Abstract or partial access establishes candidate relevance, not strong method, result, contradiction, defect, or closest-prior conclusions. Keep unresolved identifiers visibly `conflicting` or `unverified`.
- An absence claim names the full-text sections checked; otherwise write `not assessable from available access`.
- Paper-local defects are evidence inputs, not proof that a field-wide problem remains open.
- Replacement methods, module changes, new inputs, transfers, stress conditions, and new metrics belong to the explicit `$research-opportunity-mining` helper.
- Literature and synthesis are optional evidence providers for ideation, never readiness gates.
