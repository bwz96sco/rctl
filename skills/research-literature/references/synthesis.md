# Cross-paper synthesis

Produce an argued answer across papers. Use an existing paper register
and anchored paper records, or supplied sources with equivalent version,
access and claim anchors. Intermediate discovery/reading files are not prerequisites
when the supplied evidence already supports the requested comparison.

## Contract

Fix the target question/purpose, corpus, requested comparison and output. Reuse
existing project work; durable corpus work normally produces `synthesis.md` beside
`register.md`, while an ad hoc answer can stay in the requested surface. Identify
included evidence and material access/coverage gaps. Choose organization and
comparison axes that answer the question.

## Verification and completion

Lead with the direct answer and its scope. Every material conclusion traces to
paper IDs and record IDs (`<paper-id>#R<n>`) or exact source passages and versions.
For decisive inferences, check the supporting conditions and additional assumption;
conditional evidence yields a conditional conclusion. Carry corrections through
the answer and related discussion.

For empirical contrasts, state the compared operations/information and the effect
the contrast estimates. Explain incompatible settings before attributing different
outcomes to a mechanism. Contradictions are resolved by cited deciding evidence,
non-comparable under named conditions, or unresolved with the missing evidence clear.

Closest-prior work names the closest paper/tied set, exact overlap and differentiators;
a decisive uninspected candidate limits that conclusion. A field map makes missing
or ambiguous coverage visible. Add `Evidence-backed open problems` when requested,
with anchors, conditions and what the corpus leaves unanswered; this is not a
novelty verdict or investment authorization.

Complete when the requested answer is evidence-bounded, source-traceable and
honest about excluded/unresolved evidence. Account for each paper in an explicitly
assigned comparison; broader search pools need not become paper-by-paper reports.
Keep exploratory connections separate with their source basis and unverified
assumptions. A resolved discrepancy may end the inquiry.

Use paper-discovery for needed new sources and research-literature for missing
paper-local evidence. Developing questions or approaches can start without synthesis;
selection and investment decisions follow the user and project research guidance.
