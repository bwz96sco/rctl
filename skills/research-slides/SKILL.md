# Research Slides

Own the academic talk's plan and scientific content. Choose narrative, slide
organization and production methods for the audience and requested output.

## Starting contract

Establish talk type, audience/prior knowledge, purpose, time/slide allowance,
language, anonymity and requested surface: plan, notes, audit or finished deck.
Reuse supplied papers, validated claims, prior outlines/decks and project preferences.
Make evidence gaps visible and preserve the distinction between reported findings,
the presenter's interpretation and proposed work.

A plan connects the audience's question to the talk's answer. Give each slide a
clear role, supported message, approximate time and needed assets. Choose the
background, equations and method details that make that argument understandable
within the allowance. For useful distinctions between report types, consult
[talk examples](references/talk-types.md); they are options, not fixed slide recipes.

Use original scientific figures, tables and equations where they carry the evidence.
Figure creation can use research-figure; paper reading uses research-literature.
Project slide tooling owns production when applicable. Otherwise use an available
deck-production skill such as `presentations:Presentations` for PowerPoint/Google
Slides, following its actual instructions. Production proceeds when requested.

## Completion and verification

Check that the talk answers its stated question, supplies the necessary context,
supports its conclusions and fits the planned time. Verify scientific asset
content, notation, numbers, citations and qualifiers against sources. Speaker
notes and spoken claims follow the same evidence boundary. Reuse production QA.

- A plan/handoff delivers the narrative, slide outline, timing, source/asset needs
  and unresolved inputs; it requires no generated deck.
- Notes are tied to the supplied slides and evidence; an unchanged deck need not
  be rebuilt.
- An audit returns findings tied to inspected slides/sources and leaves the deck
  unchanged. Uninspected visual properties remain unassessed.
- Production/revision delivers the requested deck and checks its actual renders
  for readability, clipping, placeholders, anonymity and asset fidelity. A build
  alone establishes no visual inspection. Estimated timing is not a rehearsal.

Reuse the project note convention or bound vault's `slides/<topic-slug>/`;
without a note root use `artifacts/research-slides/<topic-slug>/`. Decks live in the
project slide directory. Managed lifecycle and acceptance stay with research-task.
