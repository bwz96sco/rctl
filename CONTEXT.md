# Research Control Language

rctl describes the agreement, evidence, and closure of one bounded research task.

## Language

**Task**: A bounded question or deliverable with a defined scope and a checkable completion condition.
_Avoid_: Run, conversation, campaign when referring to the same unit.

**Contract**: The task's question, scope, constraints, required evidence, acceptance criteria, and stopping conditions, fixed before dependent work.
_Avoid_: Execution diary, mandatory step-by-step script.

**Acceptance criterion**: A condition that must be established to complete the task; its evidence and verification method are specified in the contract.
_Avoid_: Aspirational goal, desired positive result.

**Amendment**: An explicit, reasoned change to a task's contract before the work governed by that change.
_Avoid_: Silent threshold adjustment.

**Evidence**: An inspectable observation or artifact used to support a particular finding within a stated scope.
_Avoid_: A statement that merely claims an activity occurred.

**Verification**: The recorded assessment of the contract's acceptance criteria against the task's actual materials.
_Avoid_: Successful execution, complete document structure, universal scientific certification.

**Check**: One command execution or evidence review addressing one acceptance criterion.
_Avoid_: An unscoped request to be thorough.

**Result**: The task's authoritative account of observations, interpretation, deviations, supported conclusion, and next decision.
_Avoid_: Progress update, raw run log.

**Closure**: Completion of the agreed task after its required checks pass; the finding may be positive, negative, or inconclusive.
_Avoid_: Hypothesis confirmation, end of an assistant response.

**Handoff**: A short account of the last verified progress, unresolved work, evidence locations, and next action for continuing an unfinished task.
_Avoid_: A second acceptance record.

**Current verification**: A verification still applicable to the contract and materials being considered for closure.
_Avoid_: A historical pass assumed to cover later edits.

**Reminder**: Context supplied at a host event to help the operator or agent return to the task's agreement and recorded progress.
_Avoid_: Approval, evidence, autonomous state transition.
